Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across Spokane

Heating and cooling around here covers a lot of ground: older gas furnaces in established neighborhoods, high-efficiency heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and packaged units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. Our technicians have years in the field, and we know how the weather here, the age of local homes, and local code affect the way equipment runs over time.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Repairs are usually quick: a burned contactor, a corroded flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. When we replace a system, it gets a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Tune-ups cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, and nothing does more to extend the life of your system.

Local businesses get the same attention. An overheated storefront or a drafty office costs real money, so our commercial crews focus on fast diagnosis and keep high-failure parts on board for rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment. Restaurants, retail shops, offices, and multi-unit buildings can count on us to schedule work around their business hours whenever we can.

When Your System Fails in Spokane

When your system quits, how fast help shows up matters more than anything else. That's why we keep our schedule flexible across the area and answer every call with a live person. Urgent calls typically see a technician within a day, and we treat no-heat and no-cool calls as top priority.

Common emergencies we handle include air conditioners blowing warm air, outdoor units that hum but won't start, furnaces that ignite and then shut down, breakers that trip every time the system runs, iced-over evaporator coils, clogged condensate drains backing up into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have stopped responding. Our trucks carry the capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings needed to finish most repairs in a single visit. Pricing is always upfront, so you know exactly what the work will cost before we begin.

Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Spokane

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.

Common HVAC Problems in Spokane

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
